I'm speaking at the [.NET Conference Italia 2026][1] in Milan on November 18th. My session is titled ".NET 11: non chiamatelo un semplice upgrade" (roughly, "don't call it just an upgrade"), and it opens the day right after the welcome. I'll go through what's new in .NET 11 (runtime, language, platform) and try to figure out which features are actually worth your time.

It's becoming a habit. I did [C# 12][2] and [C# 13][3] here, and last year it was [MCP servers][4]. I like doing these "what's new" talks, mostly because they force me to actually read the release notes instead of skimming them. And nothing makes you learn stuff like having to explain it to a room full of people who know it as well as you do (some better, probably).

The venue is Lodestar, in Via Fabio Filzi. It's free, but you need to register. If you're coming, find me and say hi!
