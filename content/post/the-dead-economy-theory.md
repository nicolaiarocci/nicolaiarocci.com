I’m a bit late to the party, I know, but [The Dead Economy Theory](https://www.owenmcgrann.com/p/the-dead-economy-theory) by Owen McGrann is well worth a long, careful read. It sets out – and references – the many concerns that exist regarding the social, economic and political impact caused by the advent of AI. 

What’s unsettling is that the whole argument seems to make a whole lot of sense.
